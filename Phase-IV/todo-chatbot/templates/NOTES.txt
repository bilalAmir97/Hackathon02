Thank you for installing {{ .Chart.Name }}!

Your Phase-IV Todo Application has been deployed to Kubernetes.

To access your application:

Frontend (NodePort):
  Get Minikube IP: minikube ip
  Access at: http://<minikube-ip>:30000

Backend (Port Forward):
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "todo-chatbot.fullname" . }}-backend 8000:8000
  Access at: http://localhost:8000

To check the status:
  kubectl get pods
  kubectl get services

To view logs:
  Backend:  kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/component=backend
  Frontend: kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/component=frontend
